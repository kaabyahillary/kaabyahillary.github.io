From Market Scraps to Agricultural Gold

See how our nature-driven biological cycle creates sustainable farming value.

The 4 Steps of the BSF Cycle

1

Waste Collection

We source organic food scraps, market residues, and fruit waste directly from local food hubs around Kampala, keeping public markets clean and reducing municipal landfill pressures.

2

Larvae Bioconversion

The collected biomass is fed to hungry Black Soldier Fly larvae. In just days, these highly efficient decomposers consume the waste, reducing its volume by up to 70% while rapidly packing on pure protein and healthy fats.

3

Harvesting & Processing

At their nutritional peak, the larvae are separated from the digested residues. Larvae are carefully dried and milled into stable, high-quality animal feed ingredients, while the remaining residue is cured into rich organic compost.

4

Zero-Waste Distribution

Nothing goes to waste. The protein meals go straight to poultry and fish farmers to replace expensive commercial inputs, and the nutrient-packed organic frass goes out to crop farmers to revive soil vitality.
