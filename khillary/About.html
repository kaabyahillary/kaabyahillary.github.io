Who We Are

Pioneering the circular economy in Uganda through sustainable biotechnology.

Our Story

Founded with a vision to clear organic waste from busy local trade hubs like Kalerwe, Nakasero, and Nakawa markets, VERVE GRUB bridges the gap between urban waste management and sustainable agriculture.

We harness the natural power of the Black Soldier Fly (BSF) to upcycle organic scraps into high-quality animal proteins and nutrient-dense organic fertilizers, helping Ugandan farmers lower input costs while increasing output yields.

🌱 Our Mission

To eliminate urban food waste accumulation by transforming it into premium, accessible agricultural solutions for African farmers.

🌍 Our Vision

To become East Africa's leading producer of sustainable insect-based proteins and regenerative organic soil conditioners.

VERVE GRUB

Insect-powered agribusiness inputs lowering feeding costs and restoring soil across Uganda.
