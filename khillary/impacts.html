Cleaning Our Markets, Feeding Our Soils

Urban open-air markets in Kampala generate heavy volumes of biodegradable organic garbage daily, placing heavy operational burdens on city cleaners. VERVE GRUB steps in as a decentralized solution partner.

By transforming rotting fruit mash and leafy trimmings into premium larvae proteins within a closed-loop framework, we tackle clean air sanitation concerns, local landfill overflow, and regional topsoil exhaustion simultaneously.
